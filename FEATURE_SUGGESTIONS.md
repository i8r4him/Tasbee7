# Feature Suggestions for Tasbee7 App

## High Priority (Most Useful)

### 1. **Complete Search Functionality** ⭐⭐⭐
- Currently SearchView is just a placeholder
- Search through all athkars by title or content
- Filter by sections
- Recent searches
- **Impact**: High - Makes app much more useful
Done

### 2. **Notifications/Reminders** ⭐⭐⭐
- Morning athkar reminder 
- Evening athkar reminder
- Customizable times
- Daily notifications
- Sunrise and sunshine times
- **Impact**: High - Helps users maintain daily dhikr

### 3. **Multiple Sebha Counters** ⭐⭐⭐
- Separate counters for:
  - سبحان الله (33x)
  - الحمد لله (33x)
  - الله أكبر (33x)
- Progress rings showing completion
- Auto-reset when reaching 33
- **Impact**: High - Most requested Sebha feature

### 4. **Statistics & Tracking** ⭐⭐
- Daily dhikr streak
- Total athkars read
- Total Sebha counts
- Weekly/Monthly stats
- **Impact**: Medium-High - Motivates consistency

---

### 8. **Dark Mode Support** ⭐

- **Impact**: Medium - Better UX

### 9. **iCloud Sync** ⭐
- Sync favorites across devices

- **Impact**: Medium - Multi-device support

## Recommended Implementation Order:

1. **Search Functionality** - Most requested, high value
2. **Multiple Sebha Counters** - Natural extension of current feature
3. **Notifications** - Completes existing placeholder
4. **Statistics** - Motivates daily use
5. **Widgets** - Increases visibility

---

## Quick Wins (Easy to Implement):

- ✅ Search functionality
- ✅ Multiple Sebha counters
- ✅ Reading progress (simple checkmark)
- ✅ Statistics (basic counting)

## Complex Features (Require More Work):

- ⚠️ Notifications (requires permission handling)
- ⚠️ Widgets (requires widget extension)
- ⚠️ iCloud sync (requires CloudKit setup)
- ⚠️ Audio recitation (requires audio files)
