# How to Remove Apps from App Store Connect

## Apps "In Vorbereitung zur Übermittlung" (In Preparation)

**For apps that haven't been submitted yet (like Studigo, Uni - Study Planner, Notiva):**

Unfortunately, **you cannot delete apps from App Store Connect**, even if they're in preparation. However, you have these options:

### Option 1: Leave them in "In Preparation" (Recommended)
- They won't be visible to users
- They won't appear in App Store search
- They'll just sit in your account (Apple keeps them for record-keeping)

### Option 2: Contact Apple Support
- Go to: https://developer.apple.com/contact/
- Request removal of unused app records
- Apple Support may be able to remove apps that have never been submitted

---

## Apps "Warten auf Prüfung" (Waiting for Review)

**For Tasbee7 (which is waiting for review):**

### Steps to Remove from Review:

1. **Go to your app** (Click on "Tasbee7")

2. **Navigate to "App Store" → "App-Prüfung" (App Review)**

3. **Look for "App-Version" section**

4. **Click "Remove from Review"** or **"Reject This Version"**
   - This will remove the app from the review queue
   - The app will go back to "In Preparation" status

5. **Then follow Option 1 above** (it will remain in your account but not visible)

---

## Alternative: Archive/Discontinue Apps

Since you can't fully delete them, you can:

1. **Rename them** to identify as discontinued:
   - Example: "Studigo (Discontinued)" or "Uni - Study Planner (Archived)"

2. **Leave them in "In Preparation"** - they won't be visible to anyone except you

3. **Remove any uploaded builds** if you want to clean up:
   - Go to TestFlight → Apps
   - Remove test builds if any

---

## Important Notes:

- ✅ **Apps never submitted**: May be removable by contacting Apple Support
- ❌ **Apps already submitted**: Cannot be deleted, only removed from sale
- ⚠️ **Tasbee7 is waiting for review**: You can remove it from review queue before it's approved

## Contact Apple Support:

If you want to try removing apps that have never been submitted:
1. Go to: https://developer.apple.com/contact/
2. Select "App Store Connect" → "App Management"
3. Explain you want to remove apps that were never submitted
4. Provide app names and bundle IDs
